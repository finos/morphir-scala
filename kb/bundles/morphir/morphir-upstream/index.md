# Morphir Upstream Sources

The IR specification, design and schema files mirrored from finos/morphir, worked on here and synced back.

This bundle is a **working copy, not a summary**. Everything under `sources/` is upstream's own bytes: the v4
specification prose, the design documents behind it, the JSON schemas, the BDD fixtures, the published examples and
the WIT interfaces. What [`sync.yaml`](/sync.yaml) selects, [`sync.lock.yaml`](/sync.lock.yaml) pins.

The sibling bundles are the other half of the arrangement. `morphir/morphir-ir-v4-draft` and `morphir/morphir-ir-v3`
hold *our* reading of this material — what it says, where it contradicts itself, and what we have decided about it.
This bundle holds the material itself. Bundle-relative links start at a bundle root, so those two are named rather
than linked; `kb show --bundle morphir/morphir-ir-v4-draft --path /index.md` opens one.

## Working here

```bash
.claude/skills/kb/kb sync status     # what has moved, here and upstream
.claude/skills/kb/kb sync pull       # take upstream's changes
.claude/skills/kb/kb sync diff <path>  # our upstream form against theirs
.claude/skills/kb/kb sync push --dry-run  # what an export would send
```

Two things are worth knowing before editing anything under `sources/`:

- **Markdown files carry an injected `# kb:begin` … `# kb:end` block** in their frontmatter, holding the `type` that
  OKF requires and the upstream path. Export deletes exactly those lines. Everything outside the fence — including
  Docusaurus keys like `sidebar_position` and the design docs' `tracking:` block — is upstream's and must stay
  byte-identical.
- **Everything that is not markdown is an asset**: schemas, fixtures, examples, `.mdx` pages, `_category_.yml`
  sidebar descriptors, `.wit` files. They are mirrored and synced but not parsed, and they carry no frontmatter.

Export is deliberately unfinished business: `kb sync push` writes into a checkout and stops. Branching, validating
and opening a pull request are separate, explicit steps — see the `squire` skill.
